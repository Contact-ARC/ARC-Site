Let's talk

A small team you actually get to talk to.

ARC is two founders with twelve years of combined experience. When you work with us, you work directly with the people designing and building your system.

Why ARC

ARC stands for Applications & Resource Customizations. We also chose it for what an arch is.

  • Stable

    An arch carries weight by spreading it evenly. Nothing we build should depend on a single fragile piece.

  • Lasting

    An arc keeps going. We stay with the organizations we work for and keep improving what we built.

  • Simple

    A few pieces, fitted exactly, can hold up something complicated. We aim for the simplest structure that does the job.

Small on purpose. Bigger when it counts.

Two founders means you always know who's building your system, and you can reach them directly.

When a project needs more hands or a specific skill, we bring in specialists we trust and manage the work ourselves. You get the capacity of a bigger team without losing the conversation.

What we hold ourselves to

  • Fit the organization

    Technology should adapt to people, not the other way round.

  • Say it plainly

    No jargon to hide behind. If we can't explain it simply, we don't understand it well enough yet.

  • Build it to last

    We design for the years after launch, not just the demo.

  • Keep it private

    Your systems and data are yours. We don't show what isn't ours to show.
