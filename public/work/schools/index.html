What we build for schools

Every school runs PowerSchool a little differently. These are the areas we most often work in.

  • Family and student portals

    Pages for guardians and students that show what matters to them: schedules, calendars, grades and the week ahead.

Inside the platform, not around it.

We build custom pages, plugins and reports that work within PowerSchool: they use its data, follow its permissions and look like part of the school's portal.

Staff keep the system they know. Families get something that feels built for them.

Student data stays where it belongs.

We work inside the school's environment and its access rules. Nothing we build copies student or family data somewhere it shouldn't be, and we show our work to schools as architecture, not screenshots.
